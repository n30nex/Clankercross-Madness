CLANKERCROSS MADNESS 0.3.0

Extract the Windows ZIP and run ClankercrossMadness.exe.
The game is single-player, offline, and requires no installation or account.

KEYBOARD / CONTROLLER
Throttle              W / Right trigger
Brake, then reverse   S / Left trigger
Steer                 A,D / Left stick
Lean and air control  Arrow keys / Right stick
Trick modifier        Space / A
Nitrous boost         Shift / B
Hop                   Q / X
Recover or remount    R / Y
Look around           Hold right mouse, or C + arrows / RB + right stick
Eject (sandbox)       V / LB
Pause                 Escape / Start
Menus                 Arrows and Enter / D-pad or left stick and A
Back in menus         Escape / B

SANDBOX
Choose a world, then start Sandbox. All terrain is accessible immediately.
World & sandbox settings contains weather, time of day, the day/night cycle,
gravity, tire grip, super hops and pinball landings. Nitrous is unlimited.
Zero G works best with boost and lean controls. Eject the rider for a ragdoll
flight, then recover. R/Y recovers to clear ground; the pause menu can return
you to camp. Shallow water adds drag and spray. Deep water causes a wipeout.

RACES AND TIME TRIALS
Choose a world, then Race & Time Trial. Each world has two courses.
Choose Club riders, Challengers or Experts, then start a three-lap race
against five CPU riders. Pass all 16 numbered gates in order. The route map
shows the next gate in yellow, opponents in their colors, and you in white.

R/Y returns you to the last passed gate with a five-second wait. The race
clock keeps running. You briefly pass through rivals while rejoining.
Wrong-way and missed-gate guidance appears below the riding view.
Nitrous recharges; gravity and grip use the normal riding rules. Weather
still affects traction. Pause offers a restart.

Time Trial runs one lap without opponents. Best lap, race and trial times
are stored locally. Free Ride This Track starts unlimited sandbox riding
at the selected course. There are no unlocks or required races.

TRICKS
Hold Trick + left/right lean for a whip, forward for a no-hander, or back
for a tabletop. Lean in the air without Trick to rotate into flips.
Release the controls before landing for gentle leveling assistance.

ZOMBIE ESCAPE
Pass seven checkpoint gates and the finish gate. The arrow and distance at
the top of the screen point toward the next gate. Watch the run-over counter
and grabber count. Six grabbers end the run. Jump or accelerate hard to
dislodge them. Foot zombies and bike riders replenish, and checkpoint progress
brings more enemies.

Boost has a limited reserve and recharges while released. Hops use the same
reserve, so keep some available for escaping grabbers. R/Y remounts after an
ordinary riding wipeout; attached zombies remain until shaken off. Deep water
ends the attempt. Retry from the result screen or pause menu.

WORLD AND DISPLAY
Redstone Badlands, Pinebreak Forest and Frostbite Highlands have distinct
terrain, vegetation and water hazards. Weather choices are Auto, Clear,
Rain, Thunderstorm and Snow. The day/night cycle lasts 12 minutes. Headlights
brighten automatically after dark. Graphics & sound includes detail level,
fullscreen, sound, camera shake, speed field of view, route map and frame cap.

Settings and personal best times are saved locally. There are no accounts, online leaderboards,
microtransactions or external services.
