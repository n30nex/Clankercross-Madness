CLANKERCROSS MADNESS — CREDITS

Game design, procedural bike/rider/zombie models, environment geometry,
race tracks, CPU rivals, flags, spectators, vehicle physics, crowd simulation,
interface and synthesized audio were
created for Clankercross Madness.

Generated visual assets
Layered sandstone, woodland ground, alpine snow/ice, technical rider fabric,
rutted race dirt and transparent pine foliage were created with the built-in ImageGen tool.
They are used in the game's terrain, character and vegetation materials.

Additional ground detail

Engine
Godot Engine 4.7.2, with Jolt physics and the engine's built-in font and
rendering components. Full engine and component license notices accompany
the download in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.

The game uses original branding and no extracted assets from Motocross Madness.
